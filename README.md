# Torn-OD-Insurance-Quick-Send
Small Torn Script to Quicksend items for OD insurance

How to use:

Go to Items and click on the Drug Tab (Wont work in normal Item Overview)

There will you be able to edit Names, if you want to send Ecstasy or Xanax, Player ID's, Quantity and a custom Message.
There are 4 "Profiles" you can save, with the option to delete you Custom Settings.

<img width="1184" height="345" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a8838f66-ac29-4310-880d-be45d2d3bd33" />

After filling out the settings as desired just click on the Button and the script will fill out the send option with your values, like this:

<img width="1179" height="84" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2bee2c6f-8b80-4ad0-9108-0ce962fe32ff" />

Then just click send and you are done.
